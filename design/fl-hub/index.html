FL hub: three directions

Each page draws the same fixture round and offers, with the same parts (round ring, lease dots, challenge clock, Belnap counts, escrow bar, your timeline, matches, join sheet, payouts, Advanced, and the three empty and error states). They differ in what leads and which register carries it. All three use the app's tokens from src/styles, so the one picked is what UX-1 builds.

Direction A

Dark register end to end. The live round is the hero, matches beside it. Reads as a gauge.
