Two registers, one foundation

Every surface styles against semantic tokens — --srf-*, --tx-*, --line-*, --accent — defined per register in citrate-core-tokens.css. A subtree switches register with one attribute: data-register="charter | instrument". Brand color, type, spacing, radii, and motion come from the Citrate foundation and are shared by both registers.

Green marks the consequential moment — approve, witness, settle — and nothing else. Yellow is pending and evidence. Status pills are the only pills. Numbers are always tabular, always sourced, and unknown renders as "—" with a reason, never a placeholder.

The lattice motif appears at most once per surface. Backgrounds are solid. No gradients, no colored shadows, no emoji. Locked cards invite with the one unlock action; org-scoped content is hidden, never "locked."
