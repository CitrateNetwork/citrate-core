# Membership

Membership in Citrate is represented by a soulbound token (an SBT) — a non-transferable
credential bound to your member identity. It records that you belong, and it is the key
that unlocks member surfaces across the network. Because it is soulbound, it cannot be
sold or moved to another account.

Membership is paired with a **staked SALT grant**. When you join, the network stakes a
grant of SALT on your behalf. That stake can earn yield over time from network activity.

A few points matter, and they are stated plainly on purpose:

- **The grant is a staked position, not day-one cash.** Joining does not pay you cash for
  running compute. What you receive is a staked SALT grant that can accrue yield.
- **Convertibility comes later.** The ability to convert SALT to cash is tied to mainnet,
  which is planned for a later date and is subject to a completed security audit. Listing
  of SALT on any licensed exchange is likewise pending and not promised for a given date.
- **No guaranteed returns.** Yield depends on network activity and is not a fixed or
  promised amount.

Membership onboarding runs through the desktop app. It binds this device's wallet to your
member identity so the grant funds an address you actually control, mints or confirms your
SBT, and settles the grant. Each of these is a real on-chain action that you approve
through the SignatureCeremony; nothing is credited by a local balance that was never
settled on-chain.
